My Story

As a Data Analytics Engineer, I've witnessed firsthand the incredible potential of AI and the challenges people face when trying to navigate the vast landscape of available tools. Every day, I work with data, uncovering patterns and insights that drive decisions and innovation.

The idea for Ayou came from a simple observation: while AI tools are becoming more powerful and accessible, finding the right tool for a specific task remains a significant challenge. I wanted to create a platform that not only showcases these amazing tools but also builds a community around them.

With my background in data analytics and a passion for making technology accessible, I built Ayou to be more than just a directory—it's a place where curiosity meets capability, where beginners can learn alongside experts, and where the power of AI truly becomes available for you, me, and all.

Welcome to Ayou. Let's explore the future of AI together!
